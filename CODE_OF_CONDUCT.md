# Code of Conduct

Flag Rush is a small open-source project. Contributions should keep the project useful, respectful, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep discussions relevant to Flag Rush, its code, documentation, assets, releases, or user experience.
- Respect privacy. Do not post secrets, private user data, signing files, or security-sensitive details in public threads.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized, violent, or otherwise inappropriate content.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption after being asked to stop.
- Abuse of issue, pull request, release, or security-reporting channels.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that violates this code, contact the repository maintainer privately or use GitHub's reporting tools. For security-sensitive issues, follow `SECURITY.md`.

Reports should include the relevant link, context, and any screenshots or logs needed to understand what happened. Do not include secrets or private data in public issues.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or block users when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
